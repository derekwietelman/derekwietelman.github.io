        My research career began late in my undergraduate studies as a statistics major at [Oklahoma State University](https://okstate.edu), when I realized that I cared more about analyzing the causal effects of public policy than about becoming a polling expert à la Nate Silver. I was first interested in studying how landowners and firms in energy-intensive industries respond to environmental regulation. This theme is reflected in my early work, including publications on [property value effects of Endangered Species Act listings](/research/chicken/), [voluntary third-party certifications for oil and gas projects](/research/fracking/), and [energy efficiency rules for heavy-duty trucking firms](/research/trucks/).

        ![Rapids and rocky banks of the Potomac River at Great Falls](bio/great-falls.jpg "A view of the Potomac River at Great Falls, one of my favorite places to hike in the DMV.")

        Early-career stints at [Indiana University](https://www.iu.edu) and [Resources for the Future](https://www.rff.org) confirmed my passion for research in environmental economics. In 2020, I began my doctoral studies in the [Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics](https://arec.umd.edu) at the [University of Maryland](https://umd.edu), where I was co-advised by [David Newburn](https://sites.google.com/umd.edu/dnewburn) and [Louis Preonas](https://louispreonas.com). After earning my Ph.D. in 2026, I joined the [Institute of Behavioral Science](https://ibs.colorado.edu) at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu) as a [postdoctoral associate](https://ibs.colorado.edu/people/derek-wietelman/).

        ![Two elk standing on a paved trail beside a wooden fence, with autumn aspens behind](bio/lake-estes-elk.jpg "Two Rocky Mountain elk join me for an autumn run around Lake Estes.")

        I live in the Denver metro area with my wife, four-year-old daughter, and two cats. I enjoy reading, running, baseball, and searching for kid-friendly hikes along the Front Range. I’m always happy to talk about research, collaborations, or good trail recommendations, so [feel free to reach out](/contact/).
