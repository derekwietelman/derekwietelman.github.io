Much of my research explores the pricing and allocation of energy and water resources during periods of scarcity, such as [peak electricity demand hours](/publications/ecobee/) or [severe droughts](/publications/bbr/). Currently, I am investigating how drought-induced conservation mandates interact with existing, inefficient water prices.

In this work, I document [long-run trends in surface water quality](/publications/texas/) and examine how policy design impacts nonpoint source pollution from both the [agricultural](/publications/trading/) and residential sectors. Currently, I am estimating the effects of lead remediation efforts (e.g., lead service line replacements by water utilities) on health and behavioral outcomes.
